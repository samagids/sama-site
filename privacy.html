SAMA privacy policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

SAMA is a private assistant used only by the members of one family. This policy explains what data it uses.

Where data is stored

All data (chats, memories, tasks, settings) is stored on the family's own computer. It is not sent to the developer or to any third-party server. The AI model also runs on that computer.

Sign in with Google or Apple

When a family member signs in, SAMA receives their name and email address from Google or Apple, only to check that the email belongs to someone the family administrator added. It is stored on the family computer.

Gmail and Google Calendar

If the account owner connects Gmail and Google Calendar, SAMA reads, labels and drafts email and reads and creates calendar events on the owner's behalf, on the family computer. It sends email or invites only after the owner approves each one. Google user data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. SAMA's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be removed at any time in SAMA or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Notifications

On iPhone and iPad, alerts are delivered through Apple's push service and contain no personal details unless the user turns that on.

Children

Children's accounts are created and managed by their parents, who control time limits and may review chats. SAMA does not collect children's data for any other purpose.

Deleting data

The family administrator can delete any member and all of their data from SAMA at any time.
